# QA Engineer

A QA Engineer (Quality Assurance Engineer) is responsible for ensuring that software meets functional and quality requirements before it reaches end users.

QA Engineers focus on preventing defects, validating business requirements, and improving the overall quality of software throughout the Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC).

Quality Assurance is not only about finding bugs—it is about building confidence in the product.

---

# Responsibilities

A QA Engineer typically performs the following activities:

- Analyze business requirements.
- Design test strategies.
- Create test cases.
- Execute manual tests.
- Report defects.
- Verify bug fixes.
- Perform regression testing.
- Participate in sprint planning.
- Collaborate with developers and product owners.
